## Description

Acheron is a titanic, complex super-galaxy and the final destination for all matter within [[Terminus]]. It is a "Mosaic Elliptical," defined not by a single age, but by a chaotic variety of stellar survivors. It serves as a celestial life-raft where the remnants of dozens of collided galaxies have settled into a single, dense, and functional volume.

Acheron remains the final capital for all known civilizations, as it represents the last stable concentration of baryonic matter in the observable universe. The galaxy is defined by extreme contrast; a mosaic of trillions of stars, from ancient iron-rich relics to captured younger suns, supports a civilization adapted to the constraints of the universe’s final, densest volume.

## Physical Properties

- **Stellar Heterogeneity:** Because Acheron formed from the merger of many different galaxy types (spirals, irregulars, and dwarfs), it possesses a rare "Mixed Population." You will find systems of vastly different ages and chemical compositions drifting in close proximity, creating a "patchwork" sky of varying colors and intensities.

- **The Gravitational Well:** Acheron's sheer mass acts as a stabilizer for the [[Anchor Network]]. The density of stars allows for shorter "jumps" between systems, making it the only place in the cosmos where high-speed transit and communication are truly reliable.

- **Residual Gas Pockets:** While the galaxy is mostly "Red and Dead" (lacking widespread new star formation), localized "Nursery Pockets" still exist. These are dense clouds of hydrogen harvested during the mergers that occasionally collapse to form new, precious "First-Generation Acheron" stars.
