## Description

**Penumbra** is the outermost region of [[Cosmology/Acheron/index.md|Acheron]], acting as the final transition zone between the gravitational certainty of the super-galaxy and the absolute void of [[The Dark]]. It is a region of "Stellar Dilution," where the distance between stars begins to increase exponentially and the collective glow of the galactic core fades into a distant, amber smudge.

Life in Penumbra is defined by "The Great Dimming." Without the dense energy networks of [[Cosmology/Acheron/Hyperion/index.md|Hyperion]] or the Dyson-grids of [[Cosmology/Acheron/Firmament/index.md|Firmament]], inhabitants rely on ancient, low-output red dwarfs and thermal scavenging. It is a quiet, cold, and vast territory—the graveyard of stars that were slung outward during the final galactic mergers.

---
## Physical Properties

- **Lumen-Depletion:** The ambient starlight in Penumbra is roughly 0.01% of that in the [[Cosmology/Acheron/Hyperion/index.md|Hyperion]] core. Visual navigation is impossible without high-gain sensors, and "night" is a permanent state of existence for the majority of its inhabitants.

- **The Iron-Sun Population:** Most stars in this region are "Low-Mass M-Class" dwarfs or ancient White Dwarfs that have cooled significantly. These stars are iron-rich and hydrogen-poor, making them stable for trillions of years but difficult to "rekindle" or harvest for high-energy output.

- **Vacuum-Sprawl:** Unlike the "cluttered" feeling of the inner sectors, Penumbra is characterized by its emptiness. A single system might be separated from its nearest neighbor by over five thousand light-years of true, hard vacuum, making the [[Anchor Network]] the only thing preventing total isolation.
