## Description

**Periphery Zero** is the vast, outer territory of Terminus that lies beyond the gravitational dominance of the super-galaxy **Acheron**. It is a near-perfect vacuum populated by the debris of ancient galactic collisions: rogue stars, orphaned planets, and "dead" gas clouds that failed to be pulled into Acheron.

Unlike the structured sectors of **Acheron**, **Periphery Zero** is a lawless expanse. It serves as a buffer zone between the **Acheron** and her satellites and the suicidal expansion of **The Dark**. Navigating this region requires specialized long-range propulsion, as the distances between usable matter are measured in hundreds of thousands of light-years.

---
## Physical Properties

- **Gravitational Weakness:** In this region, the "pull" of **Acheron** is minimal. Objects here drift in long, slow hyperbolic orbits. Without a local gravitational anchor (like a rogue sun), ships can easily lose their orientation and drift toward the Hubble Horizon.

- **The Ghost Star Population:** The primary landmarks of **Periphery Zero** are "Rogue Stars"—suns that were slingshot out of their home galaxies billions of years ago. These stars often harbor "Cold Worlds": planets that have survived the death of their original solar systems but now exist in total isolation.

- **Ambient Silence:** Because there is almost no gas or dust to reflect light or carry signals, **Periphery Zero** is "darker" than the interior of a galaxy. Visual navigation is impossible; pilots must rely entirely on the [[Anchor Network]] or archaic pulsar maps.

- **Metric Drag:** Because **Penumbra** sits on the edge of the **Terminus** bubble, the expansion of space-time begins to exert a "tug" on matter. Objects here experience slight anomalous red-shifts, and long-range communication requires significant signal-boosting to overcome the stretching of space.
