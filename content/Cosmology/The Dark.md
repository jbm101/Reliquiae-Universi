## Description

**The Dark** is the terminal state of the macro-universe. It is not merely "empty space," but a region of the cosmos where the metric expansion of space-time has accelerated to such a degree that matter is moving away from the observer faster than the speed of light.

As a result, any object that enters The Dark is "causally deleted." No light, signal, or physical traveler can ever return or communicate from within this void. It represents the erasure of all existence outside the **Terminus** cluster.

---
## Physical Properties

- **The Hubble Horizon:** The boundary where the expansion of space equals the speed of light. In the current epoch, this horizon has shrunk to the outer shell of **Terminus**.

- **Information Loss:** Due to the $V > c$ expansion, photons emitted from objects within **The Dark** can never reach an observer in **Terminus**. This is a "One-Way Surface"; matter can fall into The Dark, but no information can ever claw its way back out.
  
- **Absolute Zero Background:** Unlike the early universe, which had a Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) of ~2.7K, The Dark has cooled to the true floor of physics. It is a perfect cryogenic state where even the "vacuum fluctuations" of quantum mechanics appear to have gone silent.
