## Description

**Terminus** is the last "Causal Island" in the observable universe. It is a bubble of space (approximately 800,000 light-years in diameter) where the collective mass of the local galactic group has exerted enough gravitational pull to counteract the universal expansion.

While the rest of the cosmos has receded into **The Dark**, Terminus remains a stable volume where light and matter can still interact. It is the absolute limit of the known world; to pass beyond the "Terminus Fringe" is to enter a region of space expanding faster than light, resulting in immediate and permanent disconnection from the cluster.

---
## Physical Properties

- **Virial Equilibrium:** The cluster has reached a state of "gravitational stasis." The kinetic energy of the remaining satellites and star-streams is perfectly balanced by the massive gravitational potential of **Acheron**. While this prevents the cluster from flying apart, it also prevents any new matter from entering.

- **The Tidal Sheath:** At the "Terminus Fringe" space-time experiences extreme directional tension. Matter on the inner edge is pulled toward Acheron, while matter just a few kilometers further out is caught in the supraluminal expansion of the vacuum. This "sheath" acts as a physical barrier that shreds any unshielded matter attempting to cross it.

- **Baryonic Sequestration:** 100% of all detectable protons and neutrons in the observable universe are now trapped within this 800,000-light-year bubble. Because no new hydrogen gas can fall in from the intergalactic medium, the total "fuel" for star formation is finite and non-renewable.

- **Thermal Trapping:** As a "Causal Island," Terminus acts as a thermal pressure cooker. Entropy (waste heat) generated by the civilizations in **Acheron** cannot easily dissipate into the expanding void of **The Dark**. This has led to the "[[Entropy Tax]]" and strict regulations on high-energy industrial output to prevent a localized heat-death.
